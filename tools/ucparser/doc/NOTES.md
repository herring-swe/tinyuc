  0) Codepoint

  2) General Category

  5) Decomposition Type/Mapping
  6) Numeric Type/Value
  7) Continuation of 5
  8) Continuation of 5
  9) Bidi Mirrored (ignored)
  10) Unicode 1 Name (ignored)
  11) ISO Comment (ignored)
  12) Simple Uppercase Mapping
  13) Simple Lowercase Mapping
  14) Simple Titlecase Mapping (if empty: same as 9)
